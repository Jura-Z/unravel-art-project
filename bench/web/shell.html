How exactness is enforced

  • All maths is IEEE binary32 in one fixed operation order. JS rounds every operation with Math.fround; C is built with -ffp-contract=off and no fast-math, so there is no FMA or reassociation.
  • sin is a hand-written polynomial and randomness is a counter hash, so there are no platform maths libraries or shared RNG state.
  • Per-frame inputs (camera, strokes, turbulence, auto-fit) are recorded once and replayed. Checksums (FNV-1a over raw bits of position, velocity, tear state, colour and attractor state) are compared at frames 1, 10, 100 and 300.
  • WebGPU cannot promise this: WGSL allows up to 2.5 ULP error in division and square root, and may fuse multiply-adds. It is checked at frames 1 and 10 instead, because the attractor is chaotic and tiny differences grow over time.
